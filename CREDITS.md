- Logo lockup, menu art, icons, wardrobe stage and lens flare / depth of field shaders are original work for this project.
- Menu music and intro music are the in-game radio stations (see the music credits above, CC BY 4.0). The beat map in `src/ui/menu/beats.json` was measured from those files.
- UI sounds are synthesized at runtime (Web Audio), original.

## NPC archetypes (people-look track): public/assets/npc/, src/npc/arch/, src/npc/pipeline/
- 40 original NPC archetypes generated headless with Blender 5.2 + **MPFB2 / MakeHuman** (https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2): base mesh, modelling targets and the `mixamo`-named rig skeleton definition, **CC0**. (The rig only borrows the bone naming; no Mixamo assets or animations are used.)
- **MakeHuman system asset pack** (CC0, files.makehumancommunity.org): skin photo textures (repainted per archetype: tone, scalp, fades, beards, make-up), eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, hair card meshes and textures (short01-04, afro01, bob01/02, braid01, long01, ponytail01; the bun is ours, built from ponytail01), shoes01-04 meshes (recolored; sock parts trimmed).
- Garments, hats, heels, sandals, short fade hair shells, the hair strand texture, every atlas (seams, stitching, folds, denim fades, prints, insignia), micro-normal tiles and all shaders: original procedural work (`src/npc/pipeline/*.py`, `src/npc/arch/materials.js`). No real brands; police insignia and work-wear text are invented.
- Animation: NPC poses, dances, bar and kitchen work and the action clips are original and procedural (`src/npc/arch/anim.js`, `src/player/proceduralClips.js`). Since Nov 19b the NPC walk, run and base idle cycles are motion capture again: the Mixamo takes in `Xbot.glb` (walk, run, idle) and `Soldier.glb` (Walk, Run, Idle) from the three.js examples (https://www.mixamo.com, royalty-free for use in projects per Adobe's Mixamo FAQ; listed above), baked by `tools/v2/npcs/bake_mocap.mjs` into `src/npc/arch/mocap.data.js` (women / heels / seniors / brisk / jog / sprint variants are derived from those takes). `?mocap=0` = the old procedural gaits.

## Interior art kit (interiors-art: src/world/interiors/kit, fixtures/venue.js, dining.js, fashion.js, types/nightclub|bar|restaurant|cafe|lobby|boutique.js)
No third-party assets. Every surface (polished stone tile, marble, velvet, tufting, fluting, plaster, walnut, brass), the bottle shapes
and their label atlas (40 invented brands), neon signs (club names and icons), menus, art prints, garments, palm fronds and effects are
generated in code. Neon, menu and logo lettering use typefaces referenced by name from the operating system (no font files are distributed).

## Aircraft (r4-air: public/assets/vehicles/air, src/vehicles/air)
Library models from the user's collection (Models/Planes), converted by tools/v2/air/convert_planes.py. The Caloosa Skimmer 210
prop plane (prop.glb) is Plane 9, a high-wing single-prop model by BARANGER Emmanuel (helijah), made for FlightGear, GPL licensed:
credit required when shipped. The floatplane (Plane 3) and business jet (Plane 13) are user-licensed library models. Brand and
model names are invented. The airport fence, gates, signs and all aircraft effects are generated in code.

## Vehicles: utility (user downloads, Nov 19) — public/assets/vehicles/cars/utv1|atv1|utv2|golf1|bagcart1.glb
- Five downloaded models the user provided as free to use (sources kept in Models/Cars/Utility/, original authors not named
  in the downloads): a 2018 crew-cab diesel side-by-side, a 2018 sport ATV, a 2018 single-seat sport side-by-side, a
  four-passenger golf cart and a low-poly covered airport baggage cart. Changes: maker logos, model names, badge and decal
  meshes removed (lettering painted out of the textures), decimated to the car pipeline's LODs, invented names in game
  (Ridgeback Ranchhand Crew / Scrub 1000 / Dart 900, Cayo Fairway 4, Atlas Ramp Cart).
